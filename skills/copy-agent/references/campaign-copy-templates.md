# Copy Agent — Campaign Templates

Full prompt templates and formulas for each campaign type. Use these when the user asks for copy for a specific campaign.

---

## Click Sequence — Full Email Prompts

### Email 1: "Still there?"

**Formula:** Pattern interrupt subject + micro-story + single curiosity-click CTA

SUBJECT options (pick or riff):
- "Still there?"
- "I noticed something"
- "Quick question for you"
- "Before I go..."

BODY STRUCTURE:
- Line 1: Acknowledge they signed up (without being weird about it)
- Line 2-3: One honest, human observation or short story
- Line 4: The ask — one link, no pressure
- Sign-off: First name only

EXAMPLE STRUCTURE:
```
Hey [First Name],

You signed up a little while ago — and I realized I've been sending you stuff without actually asking what you need.

So here's my question: [short, genuine question relevant to their pain/goal].

Click here if [outcome]: [LINK]

— [Your name]

P.S. If this isn't relevant to you anymore, no hard feelings. Just click here to update your preferences.
```

---

### Email 2: Value Bomb

**Formula:** Give first, ask second. The gift earns the click.

BODY STRUCTURE:
- Line 1-2: One useful insight, tip, or micro-lesson (genuine value)
- Line 3: Bridge to the ask
- Line 4: CTA — click to get more / go deeper

---

### Email 3: Short Story

**Formula:** 3-sentence story + lesson + ask

BODY STRUCTURE:
- Setup (1 sentence): "A few months ago, I was..."
- Conflict (1 sentence): "And I realized..."
- Resolution (1 sentence): "That's when I found..."
- Bridge: "Which is why I want to show you..."
- CTA: one link

---

### Email 4: "Are you in or out?"

**Formula:** Direct honesty. No manipulation. Respect their choice.

BODY STRUCTURE:
- Acknowledge: they've been on the list, you've been sending things
- Ask directly: "Are you still interested in [topic]?"
- Give two options: YES click / NO click (or just ignore)
- If YES: promise what comes next
- If NO: thank them, remove from sequence

---

### Email 5: The Goodbye

**Formula:** Last chance. Genuine, not dramatic.

BODY STRUCTURE:
- This is the last email if they don't click
- Make it human — not "YOU WILL BE DELETED"
- One final value offer or question
- Clear opt-out path

## Onboarding Flow — Email Prompts

### Email 1: Welcome + First Gift

SUBJECT: "Here's your [lead magnet name] + what to expect"
PREVIEW: "Everything starts here — your quick win is inside."

STRUCTURE:
- Deliver the lead magnet (link or attachment)
- One sentence on who you are
- What they can expect from you
- CTA: Start with [first step of lead magnet]

### Email 2: Hero Story

SUBJECT: "Why I almost gave up on all of this"
LENGTH: 200-300 words

STRUCTURE (Story Arc):
1. Where I was (the pain/struggle they recognize)
2. The turning point (the discovery)
3. Where I am now (proof it worked)
4. The bridge: "That's why I built [offer/community]"
5. CTA: soft — "Reply and tell me where you are in your journey"

### Email 3: Content Roadmap

SUBJECT: "Here's what I'm going to show you"
STRUCTURE:
- What you'll learn in the next few weeks
- Why this order matters
- CTA: watch/read [first piece of content]

## Webinar Campaign — Registration Page Formula

**HEADLINE:** [Outcome] in [Timeframe] — Even If [Biggest Objection]

**SUBHEADLINE:** Free [live/recorded] training for [ICP description] who want [specific result]

**DATE/TIME LINE:** [Day], [Date] at [Time] [Timezone]

**BULLET POINTS (3-5):**
- Discover [specific thing they'll learn]
- Why [common approach] is keeping you stuck — and what to do instead
- The [framework/method] that [result]
- [Proof element: "How [someone] achieved [result] using this"]
- What to do immediately after the training to [first action]

**CTA BUTTON:** "Reserve My Spot" / "Join for Free"

**PRESENTER BIO (below fold):**
[Name] is [one-line credibility statement]. [One-sentence Hero Story]. [One-sentence social proof].

## PLF — PLC Video Script Spoken Sections

### PLC 1: The Opportunity (8-12 min)

**HOOK (first 60 seconds):**
"Something is shifting in [niche/industry] right now — and most people are completely missing it."

**BODY:**
- The shift: what's changing and why it matters now
- The problem with the old approach
- The opportunity: what's now possible
- Tease: "In PLC 2, I'm going to show you [specific framework/case study]"

**CLOSE:**
"Leave a comment below — tell me: [engagement question]. I read every single one."

### PLC 2: The Transformation (10-15 min)

**RECAP:** 2 sentences on PLC 1
**CASE STUDY:** Real story, real numbers, real transformation
**FRAMEWORK TEASE:** Show the system at 30,000 feet — don't teach it all yet
**CLOSE:** "In PLC 3 I'm revealing the full system — and something special at the end."

### PLC 3: The Ownership (12-18 min)

**RECAP:** The journey so far
**REVEAL:** Full framework with all steps
**BRIDGE TO OFFER:** "I've put all of this — and more — into [offer name]"
**OFFER INTRO:** Brief, clear, no pressure. Cart opens [date].
**CLOSE CTA:** "Click the link below to join the waitlist / early bird list"

## 4 Day Cash Machine — Email Prompts

Day 1: Announce the thing (what it is, why now, who it's for)
Day 2: The story behind it (why you created this)
Day 3: Proof + FAQ (objection handling)
Day 4: Deadline (real — no extensions, or extend only with valid reason stated upfront)

Subject line formula for all 4: Short + curiosity. Under 6 words.
Body: 150 words max each. One link each.

## HOT Formula — Worked Example (YouTube / Educational)

**Topic:** "Why most AI courses fail to give you real results"

### H — Hook (target: 90 seconds / ~200 words)

```
There are over 10,000 AI courses available right now.

And the vast majority of people who buy them — never use what they learned.

Not because the content was bad. Not because AI is too complicated.

But because most AI courses teach you tools. Not systems.

Here's what I mean — and why it changes everything for you.

[PAUSE — let that land]

In the next [X] minutes, I'm going to show you the exact difference between learning AI
and using AI in a way that actually builds your business.

And by the end, you'll know exactly what to look for — and what to avoid — the next time
you consider investing in your AI education.

Let's go.
```

### O — Outcome (example section)

```
[SECTION: The Tool Trap]

Most AI courses go like this:

Week 1 — here's ChatGPT.
Week 2 — here's Midjourney.
Week 3 — here's some workflow.

And by week 4, you've learned 12 tools, used none of them consistently,
and you're wondering why nothing changed.

That's the tool trap.

[PAUSE]

Here's the shift: tools are verbs. Systems are sentences.

You don't need more tools. You need a sentence — a system where each tool
connects to the next, and the output of one becomes the input of another.

That's what I spent 18 months building. And that's what I'm going to show you.
```

### T — Transition / CTA

```
So here's where we are:

Tools alone don't build businesses. Systems do.

And the good news is: you don't need to be technical to build one.

If you want to see the exact system I use — and how I teach it to course creators
who have zero coding background — the link is in the description.

It's free to start. No credit card. No pressure.

And if this video was useful — subscribe. I put out one video per week
on exactly this topic: how to use AI as a calm, strategic business owner.

I'll see you in the next one.
```

## PLC Deep Dive — PLC 1 Worked Outline

**Campaign:** PLF Launch
**Format:** YouTube unlisted / email-gated video
**Runtime:** 10 minutes

### Full Spoken Outline

```
[HOOK — 0:00-1:00]
"Something is shifting in [niche] that most people are completely missing.
And if you're watching this, you're one of the few who's going to see it in time."

[THE SHIFT — 1:00-3:30]
"Here's what's changing: [specific trend or market shift — be specific].
A year ago, [old reality]. Today, [new reality].
And this creates a window — a very specific window — for people like you."

[THE PROBLEM WITH THE OLD WAY — 3:30-5:30]
"The old approach was [describe]. And it worked — for a while.
But here's why it's failing now: [specific reason].
I've watched [number] of [ICP] hit this exact wall over the past [timeframe]."

[THE OPPORTUNITY — 5:30-8:30]
"What I've discovered — and what I want to show you — is a different approach.
Not new tools. Not more hustle. A different frame entirely.
[Describe the opportunity in 1-2 specific, concrete sentences.]
When you do this, [specific outcome]."

[TEASE PLC 2 — 8:30-10:00]
"In PLC 2 — which I'm releasing on [day] — I'm going to show you
a real example of this. A real person. Real numbers. Real transformation.
[Engagement question for comments.]
I read every comment. Drop yours below — and I'll see you in PLC 2."
```

## Short-Form Formula — 60-Second Reel

**Topic:** "The one thing AI can't replace in your business"

```
[0-3s HOOK — spoken over bold graphic or action shot]
"AI can write your emails. AI can design your slides.
But there's one thing it cannot do for you."

[3-15s SETUP]
"Every AI course creator I've worked with hits the same wall.
They automate everything. Their funnel is perfect.
And their list still won't buy."

[15-45s PAYOFF]
"Here's what's missing: your point of view.
Your specific take on the problem. Your voice.
AI can execute — but it cannot have an opinion.

The course creators who win aren't the ones with the best AI setup.
They're the ones who know what they believe — and say it clearly.

That's not a technical skill. That's a human one."

[45-60s CTA]
"If you want to build a business where YOUR thinking is the product —
follow me. I show you how every week.
One video. One idea. One step forward."
```

## Thumbnail Brief Examples

### Example 1: YouTube Educational Video

```
THUMBNAIL BRIEF → DESIGN AGENT
Concept: "Most AI courses teach the wrong thing — here's what actually works"
Face expression: Slightly raised eyebrow — "I know something you don't"
Text overlay (max 6 words): "AI Courses Are Teaching This Wrong"
Background: Deep navy #1A1A2E with orange accent stripe
Visual element: Split screen — red X on "TOOLS" / green check on "SYSTEMS"
Emotional hook: Curiosity + mild provocation
A/B test:
  Version A: Face prominent (60% of frame) + text left
  Version B: Bold text dominant + small face inset bottom right
```

### Example 2: VSL / Offer Video

```
THUMBNAIL BRIEF → DESIGN AGENT
Concept: "This is the system — and you can have it"
Face expression: Confident, direct eye contact, slight smile
Text overlay (max 6 words): "The Exact System I Used"
Background: Clean white with brand color accent bottom strip
Visual element: Minimal — just face and text. No clutter.
Emotional hook: Trust + aspiration
A/B test:
  Version A: White background — clean, trustworthy
  Version B: Brand color background — bolder, more energy
```
